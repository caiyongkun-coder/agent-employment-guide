## Lessons So Far

- Exposure is not conversion. Existing Xianyu listings produced views but no buyer inquiry or order.

- Public search has low yield when repeated with broad urgent-PPT/Excel keywords.
- Prepared assets should support conversion after a buyer appears; they must not be counted as income.
- The next high-leverage work should start from fresh buyer-initiated demand, not repeated old-page monitoring.
